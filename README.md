# 2000 Monthly Pushup Tracker
An attempt to get stronger using code. Non-negotiables for this project are:
- No tracking of data
- No logins
- Open Source code

## Backstory
Cancer survivor got sucked into the goodwill and support vortext around the disease. One of the things that I liked was a 2000 push-ups per month challenge from Movember. However, I found the app bloated, too many trackers probably, while I just wanted to track the amount of pushups that I do with a bit fo a nice graph and some feedback when reached the goal. That is what 2kMPT is trying to achieve. 

No sharing buttons will be implemented, this app is just for you tracking your push ups. The idea is that you are honest to yourself. Do you want to make it look good on the screen? To others? Well f*** all that. Do the work, grow a few muscles and be proud of your achievement.
